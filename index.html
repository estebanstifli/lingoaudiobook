Help and contact

Where are my books?

Download a graded-reader library in Settings › Libraries, import a file with the + button, or let the app look in the folders you choose in Settings › Browse files.

There is no voice, or no word highlighting

The app uses your phone's text-to-speech engine. Install the voice for your language in Android's text-to-speech settings. Some engines don't report word timings; then the whole sentence is highlighted.

Translation doesn't work offline

Each language's translator (about 40 MB) is downloaded once from Google ML Kit. After that, translation works without a connection.
